# OUTLINE — *Crucible of the Crimson God*

Target length: **300,000–400,000 words**. Planned as ~90–110 chapters of
~3,500–4,500 words. Outline in pencil — update as the story pulls.

Chapter file naming: `chapters/chapter-NNN.md`. Each chapter header notes
**Lu Yan's age, rank, ring count**, POV, and setting, plus a one-line goal.

---

## OVERALL ARC

- **Hook / inciting:** Chen Mo dies; wakes as a frontier infant; at six, awakens
  the Crimson Crucible — and feels the first hint that its power takes its price
  from *elsewhere*.
- **Rising complications:** survival on the frontier; first kill and first plunder;
  earning a spirit ring the hard way; the Tallyman; leaving Slagford.
- **Midpoint reversal:** a near-death at a Title-tier hand survived only by a
  monstrous blood-sacrifice — proof of both his power and its displaced cost; the
  thing that hardens him and the thing he can no longer pretend not to see.
- **Crisis / low point:** the cost comes due on someone he refuses to lose; the
  warband / the people under his banner pay for his ascent; he confronts what the
  "no drawback" really meant.
- **Climax:** the confrontation everything built toward — Spirit Hall / a rival war
  god / the divine seat — won not by raw power but by the limit he *chose* to grow.
- **Resolution:** the order he forges and the man he decided to be inside it.

## ARC 1 — ASH & AWAKENING (Slagford; age 6–10) — ch. 1–14

Goal: establish world, voice, the Crucible, the ruthless engine, and the seed of
its true cost; earn ring #1; take down the local power (the Tallyman); leave.

- **Ch.1 — The Cold Road.** Chen Mo's death; rebirth; six years compressed; the
  awakening rite at Slagford's tallhouse. Innate Full Spirit Power. The Crucible
  manifests wrong — crimson, unclassifiable; the examiner's fear. First plunder
  (instinctive, on a dying rat-beast) and the grass that dies and does not regrow.
  *Turn:* he understands what he is. *Hook:* the examiner sends a letter east.
- **Ch.2 — Slag and Cinder.** Orphan life; Old Cinder the smith; the forging
  instinct; Mei Sang; Ke Lang's bullying. Lu Yan's cold calculus. *Hook:* the
  Tallyman's men take the season's "tithe of talent" — awakened children.
- **Ch.3 — The Tithe.** He hides his rank; the politics of the frontier; first
  on-page ruthlessness (turning the bully into a tool, not killing). *Hook:* to
  earn a ring he must enter the Ashwood.
- **Ch.4 — Ashwood.** First hunt; the war-trance as cold clarity; the kill; the
  hundred-year ring choice — and a harder, riskier ring he chooses instead.
- **Ch.5 — First Ring.** Absorption; first soul skill; the displaced-cost beat
  sharpens (what wilts around him). Old Cinder's warning. *Hook:* the Tallyman
  noticed the dead beast.
- **Ch.6–8 — The Tallyman.** Escalation; Lu Yan builds his first tiny "banner"
  (Ke Lang + others); plunders a lieutenant; the midpoint-of-arc reversal (a
  beating, a captivity, a sacrifice). Mei Sang's line in the sand.
- **Ch.9–11 — The Forge Turns.** He takes the Tallyman's operation by force and
  guile; first real kill of a man and first human plunder — and what it costs the
  town, not him. The Bishop scout's letter bears fruit: Spirit Hall is watching.
- **Ch.12–14 — Leaving Slagford.** He outgrows the frontier; a choice about Mei
  Sang and Old Cinder; departs with a handful of sworn followers and ring #2.
  *Arc hook:* the wider world — academy, empire, Spirit Hall.

## ARC 2 — THE BANNER UNFURLED (academy / warband; age 11–14) — ch. 15–32
Enters a regional academy (not Shrek yet) or a frontier military college; outclasses
peers; forms his warband proper; first clashes with Spirit Hall's reach; rings 3–4;
the war-domain matures; the cost becomes undeniable to those close to him.

## ARC 3 — IRON AND BLOOD (wildlands conquest; age 14–17) — ch. 33–52
Mercenary conquest; beast-tides; rivals plundered; spirit bones won; **midpoint
reversal** (near-death vs. a Title Douluo, survived by monstrous sacrifice).
Betrayal; the warband fractures over what he is.

## ARC 4 — THE TALLY OF GODS (empire politics; age 17–20) — ch. 53–70
Entangled in Star Luo / Heaven Dou / Spirit Hall politics; builds a true territory
and proto-sect; Spirit Hall escalates to open enmity; the displaced cost reaches
the land itself.

## ARC 5 — WAR (large-scale; age 20–23) — ch. 71–88
Open war; the War-Banner at full scale; **crisis/low point** — the cost comes due
on someone he won't lose; he confronts the meaning of "no drawback."

## ARC 6 — THE RED PRIEST (ascension; age 23+) — ch. 89–105
The path to Title Douluo and the divine trials; the endgame antagonist; **climax**
won by the limit he chose to grow; **resolution** — the order he forges.
