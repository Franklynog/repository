# SKYRIM.html

*The Elder Scrolls, more or less.* An entire open-world action RPG in a single,
dependency-free HTML file.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. That's it. No build, no install, no 14 GB download.

## Features

- An open world: snowy mountains, a winding river (with one bridge, as is tradition),
  pine forests, a town, a draugr-infested barrow, and a bandit camp
- Real-time combat — melee swings, stamina, knockback, floating damage numbers
- **FUS RO DAH** — a dragon shout on cooldown that sends everything in front of you flying
- Leveling, XP, gold, and health potions looted from your enemies
- NPCs with dialogue, including a guard who used to be an adventurer like you
- A main quest: speak with the Jarl, then climb the Throat of the World and slay the
  dragon that roosts there (it flies and breathes fire; bring potions)
- Death sends you to Sovngarde briefly, then respawns you in town minus half your gold

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `WASD` / arrows | Move |
| `Space` | Attack |
| `F` | Shout (FUS RO DAH) |
| `E` | Talk / interact |
| `H` | Drink health potion |

## Honest disclaimers

- Radiant AI is implemented as `Math.random()`
- The economy has one currency and zero shops
- You cannot put a bucket on anyone's head (yet)
