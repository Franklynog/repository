Crimson Sequence

A Soul Land story — the climb of the ninth Sequence

In a world where a soul master's worth is read from a crystal, six-year-old Huo Yuan's crystal reads zero — because his power was never a martial soul at all. It is a Sequence: the shattered inheritance of a dead war god, climbed one dangerous role at a time, from Hunter to Red Priest. To finish the climb he must out-hunt the cult that murdered his father, out-scheme Spirit Hall, and out-act the madness that consumed every climber before him.

Soul Land / Douluo Dalu AU · power system inspired by the Red Priest pathway from Lord of the Mysteries · rated T (violence, character death) · updates serially · 3 chapters posted
