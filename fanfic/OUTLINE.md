# OUTLINE — *Crimson Sequence*

Target: ~105 chapters × ~3,500 words ≈ 350k–380k words.
Outline in pencil: Vol 1 is fully beat-mapped; later volumes are mapped at decreasing
resolution and get fully beat-mapped one volume ahead of drafting.

## Global arc
- **Hook:** the zero-reading awakening; the shard chooses him; father murdered (ch 3).
- **Rising:** climb Sequences 9→6 while dodging Spirit Hall and being "ranched" by the
  Covenant, who quietly kill his other enemies for him (horror of being livestock).
- **Midpoint (ch ~55, Vol 4):** triple revelation — his mother was murdered as a shard
  custodian; the Covenant has been *protecting* him; the climb ends with him devoured
  (or devouring). The game changes from "get strong" to "break the cycle."
- **Crisis (Vol 5, ch ~74–76):** Old Ge dies; Yuan goes Reaper-deep into the Tide on a
  battlefield; nearly kills surrendered prisoners; Qingyi barely calls him back. His army
  fears him; he fears himself; he disbands and walks away — the seat looks unwinnable.
- **Climax (Vol 7):** at rank 99 with all nine shards, in the god-trial, Wuhui makes his
  devouring bid through the seat itself; Yuan defeats him not by conquering but by
  *refusing the role* at the final step — the first climber to break character on purpose.
- **Resolution:** the seat reforged as the **Hearthkeeper's seat** (guarding flame, not
  conquering fire); cost paid; epilogue years later in rebuilt Lieshan.
- **Relationship beats (Yuan/Qingyi):** meet ch 14 → grudging alliance Vol 2 → near-miss
  + interrupted confession Vol 3 → rupture (he uses her caravan in a Conspirer scheme
  without telling her) Vol 4 → she returns as war-healer, commitment ~ch 70 Vol 5 →
  steady partnership Vols 6–7; she is present at the seat-breaking.

## VOLUME 1 — HUNTER (ch 1–12) · Yuan age 6→12 · Lieshan & the road
*Volume goal: orphan the boy, forge the hunter, get him to Yanshui with shard #1,
two rituals' knowledge, his first soul ring, and a debt-ledger with the Covenant's
name at the top.*

1. **The Sixth Birthday** — Awakening ceremony; crystal reads zero; village scorn;
   that night the shrine hill burns red, the shard brands his palm, the voice names
   him *Hunter, Sequence Nine — S-rank, physical*. Turn: trash → secretly chosen.
   Hook: Old Ge sees the brand and goes grey in the face.
2. **The Tanner's Price** — Old Ge corners him; tests the brand with fire; reveals the
   bare minimum (a path, a price, a rule: *act the hunt, never become the kill*).
   Training begins disguised as tannery chores. Ledger habit starts. Meanwhile Deacon
   Qian Mo files the anomaly report. Hook: grey-robed strangers on the forest road
   asking about "children with red dreams."
3. **Smoke on the Treeline** — Covenant scouts raid Lieshan for the shard. Huo Tieshan
   dies holding the lane. Yuan's first human kill — a trap, a knife, a hunter's kill,
   not a warrior's. They burn the dead. In father's iron chest: discharge letter naming
   Ash Hollow, and a grey-iron token. Turn: home → ashes; boy → hunter with a debt.
   Hook: Old Ge: "They'll mark the village answered. Then they'll come back to count."
4. **What the Forest Charges** — Old Ge and Yuan relocate to a forest blind; grief
   processed through craft; rules of the acting principle taught properly; first
   Tide-flicker (he stalks a deer and almost can't stop). Hook: the brand itches at night
   — something big has marked *them*.
5. **Marked Prey** — The thing hunting them: a Covenant hound (beast-grafted tracker).
   Cat-and-mouse across three days, Yuan as bait. They kill it; in its collar, a
   tally of villages. Turn: prey → predator. Hook: hound collars come in pairs.
6. **The Datura Hollow** — First soul ring: 400-year Datura Snake, taken in-role (a
   two-day solo stalk). Ring dissolves into flesh: venom-sense + joint flexibility
   flavor his Hunter abilities. Soul power breaks rank 11. Hook: Old Ge, watching him
   absorb it wrong-and-right at once, finally says the word "Sequence" aloud.
7. **The Custodian's Story (I)** — Old Ge's partial confession: the pathway, the nine
   shards, the rule of gates, what the Covenant farms climbers for (partial — he omits
   his own climb). Yuan writes the first page of the grudge-ledger. Hook: to ever climb
   past Hunter he needs shard #2's Rubric — and Old Ge doesn't know where it is. But the
   shard in Yuan's palm tugs *east*. (East = toward Yanshui.)
8. **Winter Count** — Time-skip montage chapter (ages 7–10) structured as ledger
   entries: hunts, near-misses, two more Covenant "census" visits to the region that
   they hide from, soul power to rank 17. The second hound finds them. Turn: the blind
   is blown. Hook: Old Ge: "You're done growing here. Yanshui has an academy and a
   crowd to vanish into. And the tug is stronger every year, isn't it?"
9. **The Long Road East (I)** — Road chapter: refugee columns (Spirit Hall tithes rising
   — canon-era pressure shown ground-level), bandit toll-bridge solved Hunter-style
   (traps, fear, zero deaths — practicing restraint against the Tide). Hook: a grey robe
   in the refugee column, watching Yuan a beat too long.
10. **The Long Road East (II)** — The grey robe is a Covenant *assessor*, not assassin;
    it tests Yuan with a staged crisis (lets bandits take a child hostage to watch him
    work). Yuan saves the child, clocks that he was watched, doesn't catch the assessor.
    First taste of being studied like stock. Turn: he realizes some enemies are *graders*.
    Hook: assessor's report (antagonist interlude, 1 page): "Candidate viable. Shepherd
    accordingly. Remove the lame custodian when convenient."
11. **Yanshui Gates** — Arrival; city worldbuilding; money problem; Yuan hunts marsh
    soul beasts for the bounty board to fund academy entry; rank 19, Sequence 9 fully
    digested (signs: the role no longer "fits" — it *is* him — bible rule for readiness).
    Hook: academy entrance trials in three weeks; crystal-reading is the first gate —
    and his crystal reads zero.
12. **Zero Through the Gate** — Entrance trials: fails crystal, demands the combat
    alternative (obscure academy bylaw Old Ge knew); beats three seniors with traps and
    terrain inside the dueling ring; admitted as "physical-class special intake" —
    public humiliation + public legend in one stroke. Volume close: enrolled, hidden,
    hunted, and the palm-shard tugging toward someone *inside the academy*.
    Hook → Vol 2.

## VOLUME 2 — PROVOKER (ch 13–28) · age 12→14 · Yanshui Academy
*Goal: shard #2, Sequence 8 ritual, Qingyi & Beichen enter, Qian Mo closes in, the
"only one climber" rule lands.* Key beats: meet Qingyi (14) over a market debt; rivalry
with Beichen (15); the tug leads to the academy archive — shard #2 is set in the hilt of
a trophy saber (21); Provoker ritual needs a ring "taken in a duel of words" — he must
talk a 700-yr Mandrill into single combat (23–24); inter-academy tournament where Qian Mo
attends specifically to crystal-test contestants (25–27); Yuan wins ugly, dodges the
test by public forfeit at the final — chooses obscurity over glory (character-defining);
Old Ge reveals "only one climber per era" and that the Covenant killed this era's other
candidate *for* Yuan (28, volume hook: their protection is a leash).

## VOLUME 3 — PYROMANIAC (ch 29–46) · age 14→16 · Star Dou & the frame
*Goal: shard #3, fire, first Tide crisis, Qian Mo's murder pinned on Yuan, Shrek cameo,
Old Ge's full confession.* Beats: graduation expedition into Star Dou (31–35); Shrek
Seven Devils encounter (38–39, brief); Pyromaniac ritual mid-volume — ring from a
10,000-yr Flame Lion cub's *mother*, taken protecting the cub (inverts the hunt; fire
serves guarding — seeds the ending); Covenant kills Qian Mo and frames Yuan (40);
fugitive arc; first full Tide episode burning a Covenant waystation (43) — he scares
himself; Old Ge's history + the dead partner (44); near-miss with Qingyi before he
flees Yanshui as an outlaw (45–46).

## VOLUME 4 — CONSPIRER (ch 47–62) · age 16→18 · Heaven Dou intrigue
*Goal: the midpoint engine. Shards #4–5, Conspirer ritual, mother-truth, Mu Heng's
betrayal, Spirit Hall obsession begins, Qingyi rupture.* Beats: outlaw-to-asset for a
Heaven Dou spymaster (49); clears his own name via a year-long scheme (50–54);
**midpoint ch 55:** Covenant archive raid reveals mother's custodian bloodline, her
murder, and the devouring doctrine; Conspirer ritual — ring must be taken "by a plan the
prey walks into willingly" (57); Mu Heng betrays the safehouse (58); Yuan's counter-plan
used Qingyi's caravan as unwitting bait — it works, and she finds out (60); rupture (61);
volume ends with war declared (Spirit Hall vs empires, canon-era) and Yuan, alone by his
own hand, enlisting under a dead man's name (62).

## VOLUME 5 — REAPER & IRON-BLOODED KNIGHT (ch 63–80) · age 18→21 · the war
*Goal: two sequences, the low point, the commitment.* Beats: ranker-to-commander rise;
Beichen returns as his vanguard (65); Reaper ritual on a battlefield (67); harvest
mechanics horrify and tempt; Qingyi reappears running a healers' column (68–69);
reconciliation earned across sieges, commitment ~70; Knight ritual (73); **Old Ge dies
holding Redford Gate (74); Tide catastrophe (75–76)** — stopped one breath short of the
unforgivable line by Qingyi reading his ledger aloud; he disbands his command, walks
into the ash (volume ends at his lowest, 80).

## VOLUME 6 — WAR BISHOP & WEATHER WARLOCK (ch 81–94) · age 21→23 · the hunt reversed
*Goal: turn the leash around — hunt the Covenant; gather shards #6–8; Mu Heng's
defection and death; Ash Hollow falls.* Beats: father's grey-iron token opens Covenant
safehouses (callback); Bishop ritual blessing a *refugee* column, not an army (84);
Mu Heng defects with the Hollow's map, dies covering the approach (88–89); Weather
Warlock ritual inside the caldera storm (91); Ash Hollow assault (92–94) — Wuhui's
body destroyed but his ghost rides the seat-heart shard #9 *into Yuan's keeping*:
poison victory. Hook: to reach 99 and the trial, he must carry his devourer with him.

## VOLUME 7 — CONQUEROR & RED PRIEST (ch 95–105) · age 23→24 · the seat
*Goal: climax & resolution.* Beats: Conqueror ritual and the temptation arc — his
domain makes obedience *pleasant*, the most seductive Tide yet (96–98); rank 99 (100);
the god-trial: replay-gauntlet of his nine roles where Wuhui wears the faces of his
dead (101–103); the refusal: he breaks character at Sequence 0, shatters the cycle,
reforges the seat as Hearthkeeper (104); epilogue: rebuilt Lieshan, the shrine rebuilt
around an ordinary hearth, the red star gone from the sky; the ledger's last page:
all debts marked paid (105).
