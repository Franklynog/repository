## 1. Premise & Tags

**Logline:** At six-year-old Huo Yuan's awakening, his martial soul manifests as a
faceless priest in wound-red robes — and the crystal beneath his hand reads zero. Spirit
Hall files him as a powerless freak: an idol soul with nothing in it. But the figure is
no martial soul. It is the Vestige — the shadow of a dead war god's shattered seat, cast
through his flesh — and the power to fill that shadow must be climbed one dangerous role
at a time, from Hunter to Red Priest. To finish the climb he must out-hunt the cult that
murdered his father, out-scheme Spirit Hall, and out-act the madness that consumed every
climber before him.

- **Fandom:** Soul Land / Douluo Dalu (anime/novel), AU parallel to canon era
- **Inspiration:** Power system inspired by the Red Priest pathway from *Lord of the
  Mysteries* (Sequences, acting principle, loss-of-control), translated into Soul Land terms
- **Canon divergence:** AU from before canon. Set in the same generation as Tang San.
  Canon events (Shrek Academy, Spirit Hall's rise) occur in the background; canon cameos
  are brief and respectful — this is Huo Yuan's story, not a canon rewrite.
- **Rating:** T → M in war arcs (battle violence, death of named characters, war atrocities
  depicted as costly, no gratuitous gore, no sexual content)
- **Ship:** Huo Yuan / Lan Qingyi (OC×OC, slow burn, commitment in Vol 5)
- **Tropes:** weak-to-strong, hidden power, found family, mentor's death, slow burn,
  battle-madness as a cost, "act the role to become it"
- **Content warnings:** parental death (ch 3), war, on-page deaths of named characters
- **Target:** ~105 chapters, ~3,500 words each, 300k–400k total

## 2. The Power System (HARD RULES — do not bend)

### The Crimson Sequence
Nine hundred years ago the **Crimson God of War** fell; his god seat shattered into
**nine shards**. Each shard carries a **Rubric** — the ritual recipe for one Sequence.
A mortal who performs the rituals in order climbs the dead god's path:

| Seq | Name | Soul-power band (gate) | Core grants |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Hunter** | ranks 1–19 | Predator's physique (strength/speed/senses ×3 mortal), trap-craft intuition, fire resistance, "Marked Prey" (once marked, he can track a target anywhere within ~10 km) |
| 8 | **Provoker** | 20–29 | Incite or quench emotion in others (rage, fear, fervor), voice carries over battlefield, reads intent in body language |
| 7 | **Pyromaniac** | 30–39 | Generates/commands fire from his own blood-heat, body partial-ignition, explosion shaping; immune to flame below soul-beast-fire grade |
| 6 | **Conspirer** | 40–49 | Schemes "settle" into near-precognitive plans; can sense when he himself is part of someone's scheme; lies become very hard to detect |
| 5 | **Reaper** | 50–59 | Conjured crimson scythe; "harvest" — each kill in battle feeds strength/healing to him for minutes; terror aura |
| 4 | **Iron-Blooded Knight** | 60–69 | Body becomes living war-steel (Soul Douluo physique without rings), weapon mastery saturation, wounds close in combat |
| 3 | **War Bishop** | 70–79 | Bless/curse armies (morale, stamina, edge-keeping as real buffs), consecrate ground, war rituals |
| 2 | **Weather Warlock** | 80–89 | Calamity weather over kilometers (firestorm, ash winter, red fog); flight on thermal columns |
| 1 | **Conqueror** | 90–98 | Domain of Conquest: within it his word is physical law to the defeated; armies' strength partially flows to him |
| 0 | **Red Priest** | 99 → god seat | Godhood. Whoever holds all nine shards at rank 99 may attempt the seat — or be eaten by it. |

### Hard limits
1. **Rank gates are absolute.** He cannot ritual up a Sequence until his soul power
   reaches the band's floor — and his soul power only grows the normal way: cultivation
   and soul rings (see §below). No skipping. Ever.
2. **A ritual needs three things:** the **Rubric** (read from the shard), **catalysts**
   (heart-blood of a specific soul beast type + rare materials, listed per ritual when it
   appears in prose — record here), and a **soul ring taken in-role** (a stalk-and-kill
   for the Hunter, a duel of words for the Provoker, etc.). A ring taken out-of-role still
   gives soul power but does NOT count for the ritual.
3. **The acting principle:** between rituals he must *live the role* to "digest" it.
   Forcing the pace, or acting against the role's nature for too long, raises the
   **Crimson Tide** — battle-madness. Tide stages: red-rimmed vision → auditory drumbeat
   → loss of language → berserk. If he ever kills a defenseless innocent while lost in
   the Tide, the digestion corrupts permanently (this is what broke previous climbers).
   Anchors against the Tide: his ledger habit (counting), mother's pendant, later Qingyi.
4. **The Vestige & the zero reading.** His martial soul *manifestation* is the
   **Crimson Vestige**: a faceless priest, a door-and-a-half tall, robes "the red of a
   wound gone old," gauntleted hands folded graveside-fashion, wind goes around it, smell
   of cold incense and hot iron (all est. ch 1). It is the shadow of the god's seat cast
   through his flesh — a *shape his power will fill*, not a container of power. Hence the
   crystal reads **soul power: zero**; Spirit Hall's file says "idol-type tool soul,
   unidentified; powerless." He cannot summon it at will at Sequence 9 (tried ch 1; the
   path answered *climb*). **Manifestation schedule:** it appears on its own at each
   Sequence ritual and at mortal extremity, growing more solid and detailed with every
   Sequence climbed — a visible ladder of his progress; voluntary summoning unlocks at
   Sequence 7; at Sequence 0 it is "no shadow." His true soul power gates his climbs but
   nests in his flesh: no soul tool, ring-assessment, or seal designed for martial souls
   works on him — including healing arts that target the martial soul. Blessing: his
   *strength* is unweighable. Curse: his *face* is memorable — fifty-one villagers saw
   the red priest, and Spirit Hall has it on paper.
5. **Rings fuse into flesh, not soul.** When he absorbs a spirit ring, it dissolves into
   his body: no visible ring, no discrete ring-ability. Instead the beast's nature flavors
   his current Sequence powers (recorded per ring below). Ring age limits still apply to
   his effective rank as normal.
6. **Shards attract each other — and their holders.** Within ~50 km, a carried shard
   pulls toward others like a compass and *can be felt doing so by other sequence-bearers
   and by Patriarch Wuhui's hounds.* Carrying shards is carrying a beacon.
7. **Only one climber.** Two people cannot digest the same Sequence era. The Covenant
   doesn't want to stop him — Wuhui needs a climber to ripen, then be devoured.

### Rituals performed (running log)
- **Sequence 9 — Hunter:** performed at age 6 (ch 1), *involuntarily* — triggered by
  awakening ceremony + shard #1 hidden under the village shrine. He didn't brew it;
  the shard chose him. (This is the series' opening mystery: WHY him — answer in Vol 4:
  his mother's bloodline; see Secrets.)

## 3. Characters

### Huo Yuan (MC)
- **Born:** same year as Tang San (canon-parallel). Age 6 in ch 1.
- **Appearance:** black hair, dark-amber eyes that catch red in firelight; wiry then
  increasingly solid build; small crescent burn-scar on right palm from the shard
  (the **Hunter's Brand** — itches near soul beasts and Covenant members, est. ch 1).
- **Family:** father **Huo Tieshan** — ex-sergeant, Heaven Dou border army, 12 years'
  service, commended for "the action at Ash Hollow against the grey heresy"; missing top
  joint of left ring finger; weapons: war bow + boar spear; dies ch 3 (age-7 raid) after
  killing two raiders and marking the leader (arrow driven into shoulder). Dying words
  establish that Yuan's counting habit is *inherited from him* ("Count me"). Mother
  **Shen Wan** (died of "wasting illness" when Yuan was 4 — *actually* poisoned by the
  Covenant; secret, revealed Vol 4).
- **Voice/manner:** quiet, watchful, dry. Counts things compulsively — arrows, coins,
  debts, kills (his **ledger**, a real battered notebook from ch 2 on; also his anchor).
  Speaks in short sentences; goes formal when furious.
- **Want:** keep what's his alive. **Flaw:** keeps a ledger of grudges too; pays every
  debt, fair or cruel; slow to trust. **Arc:** hunter who kills to protect → conspirer
  who uses people → conqueror tempted to *own* what he protects → priest who learns
  guarding ≠ possessing. Ending: he breaks/reforges the seat rather than inherit it.
- **Knows (vs reader):** does not know mother was murdered (reader learns Vol 4 with him);
  does not know Old Ge's full history until Vol 3.

### Ge Lan, "Old Ge" (mentor)
- Tanner of Lieshan Village; ~70s; left leg ruined, walks with an iron-shod staff.
- **Truth:** failed climber of the *previous era* — reached Sequence 7 (Pyromaniac) sixty
  years ago, lost his partner to the Tide, mutilated his own meridians to stop climbing,
  and became custodian of shard #1. Has been *waiting* in Lieshan because the shard was
  buried here. Sardonic, hoards tea, calls Yuan "cub."
- **Arc:** guilt → second fatherhood → dies in Vol 5 (ch ~74) holding a gate so Yuan's
  refugees escape; his death is what tempts Yuan deepest into the Reaper's Tide.

### Lan Qingyi (love interest)
- Daughter of a Yanshui City caravan family. Yuan's age. Martial soul: **Azure Frost
  Lotus** (control/healing; ice petals that numb pain and slow bleeding — her healing
  works on Yuan because it treats *flesh*, not martial soul; this matters, see rule 4).
- Sharp-tongued, ruthless at haggling, secretly keeps her own ledger (parallel habit —
  she and Yuan discover this about each other in Vol 2, it becomes "their thing").
- **Arc:** merchant pragmatist → battlefield healer → the person who can call him back
  from the Tide by *reading his ledger aloud*. Slow burn: meet ch 14, alliance Vol 2,
  near-miss Vol 3, rupture over a Conspirer scheme Vol 4, commitment Vol 5 (~ch 70).

### Xue Beichen (rival → brother-in-arms)
- Academy rival, Silver Wolf martial soul (agility/attack). Proud, honorable, loud where
  Yuan is quiet. NOT the betrayer. Loses to Yuan in Vol 2 tourney, demands rematches for
  years; becomes his vanguard commander in the war arcs.

### Mu Heng (the betrayer)
- Affable senior student in Yanshui, twin-saber soul master, everyone's friend.
  **Secret:** Covenant-planted ("a soft hand inside the schools"). Genuinely comes to
  like Yuan — betrays him anyway in Vol 4 (ch ~58) out of fear of Wuhui, then defects
  with vital intel in Vol 6 and dies making it count. Track carefully: every kindness
  Mu Heng shows pre-betrayal must read true on reread.

### Antagonists
- **Deacon Qian Mo** (Vols 2–3): Spirit Hall deacon for the Yanshui circuit. Bone Crow
  martial soul. Not a cartoon: a believer who thinks unregistered power = future massacre.
  Files the first "anomalous child" report (planted ch 2). Killed by Covenant in Vol 3
  and the murder pinned on Yuan — the frame that drives Vol 3's back half.
- **The Ashen Covenant:** cult of the dead god's failed clergy. Grey robes, ash-tattooed
  tongues, fight with hook-chains and beast-grafted "hounds." Doctrine: ripen a climber,
  devour him at Sequence 1, restore the Patriarch.
- **Patriarch Wuhui ("No-Regret")**: the previous era's Sequence 1 Conqueror, 800+ years
  old, body long dead, persists as a sequence-ghost bound to the largest shard (#9,
  the seat-heart). Final antagonist. Speaks gently. Has eaten eleven climbers.
- **Spirit Hall** (institutional): background per canon; Bibi Dong era. Yuan's existence
  (undetectable power) becomes a Spirit Hall obsession from Vol 4.

### Canon characters (cameo discipline: brief, in-character, never upstaged)
- Tang San & Shrek cast: one Star Dou Forest encounter, Vol 3 (~ch 38–39), no power
  reveals exchanged, mutual "that one is dangerous" respect.
- Grandmaster (Yu Xiaogang): a corridor conversation about "souls the crystal cannot
  read" — he theorizes, never learns the truth.

## 6. Locations
- **Lieshan Village:** forty households on the Star Dou Forest's eastern fringe, Heaven
  Dou Empire. Shrine on the hill (shard #1 was beneath it). Old Ge's tannery by the creek
  (stink of oak-bark liquor; established ch 2).
- **Yanshui City:** border trade city, 10 days east. Bluestone walls, canal market.
  Home of the **Yanshui Martial Academy** (Vol 2) and Qian Mo's Spirit Hall branch.
- **Star Dou Forest:** per canon.
- **Ash Hollow:** Covenant seat, location unknown to Yuan until Vol 6 (a caldera
  monastery north of the Sunset Plains). Named in Huo Tieshan's discharge letter (ch 3).

## 8. Style sheet
- 3rd person limited, past tense, Yuan POV default; rare single-scene other-POV
  (antagonist interludes ≤1 per volume, labeled).
- Numbers Yuan counts are always given exactly ("eleven arrows, three good").
- Sequence names capitalized; the Tide always "the Crimson Tide" or "the Tide"; the
  manifestation always "the Vestige" (Yuan's father called it "the tall fellow,"
  the village says "the ghost-priest" — keep all three registers).
- Chinese-style naming, family name first (Huo Yuan, Lan Qingyi).
- Chapter headers: `Chapter N — Title` + volume tag + Yuan's age on first chapter of
  each in-story year.
