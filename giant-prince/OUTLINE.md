## OVERALL ARC

- **Hook / inciting:** Xu Qing dies crossing *beyond the timescape*; a purple light
  drags his soul into a dying seventeen-year-old in the Seoul slums; the System brands
  him the **Loki-class — Giant Prince of Elbaf**. The quietest survivor alive is handed
  the loudest power in the world.
- **Rising complications:** he learns to grow without being seen; first kills, first
  Gates, first **Giant Feats**; a misclassified "weak E-rank" who keeps surviving the
  unsurvivable; the Association, guilds, and the legacy's hungry will all start to pull.
- **Midpoint reversal (Arc 4):** he and **Sung Jin-Woo** recognize each other as the
  two things the System cannot contain; the world stops being a frontier he can hide
  on. Scale has found him. He can no longer stay small.
- **Crisis / low point (Arc 7 open):** the price comes due — the legacy nearly eats
  the man; the attention his size bought brings a power he cannot out-calculate; the
  warmth he guarded (his mother's memory, the few he protects) is what's on the table.
- **Climax:** a threat that *requires* full giant-prince power and the **`[True Giant
  Prince]`** state — won not by raw size but by the **limit he chooses to grow**,
  fusing the cold survivor and the mythic giant on his own terms.
- **Resolution:** what he decides to be — neither the unseen rat of the Hollows nor
  the throned prince the legacy wanted, but a third thing he forged himself; and the
  first true sight of what the **timescape** is, and that it is not done with him.

## PROLOGUE — *The Light Past Time* (~3,000)
*Beyond the timescape:* Xu Qing's last moment in his old world — old, cold, exact,
stepping past the final boundary of time into the nothing that even gods fear, because
caution had carried him as far as caution could and the only way left was *through*.
The crossed pupils. A **purple light** that is not of his world reaches *back through
the nothing* and takes him. Mythic, hushed, dread-bright. **Hook:** he wakes choking on
another body's blood, in another world's rain, and something vast and patient says
*"...little prince."*

## ARC 1 — REBIRTH & GIANT AWAKENING (the Hollows, Seoul; ~weeks) — ch. 1–12
Goal: land the soul in the body and the world; reveal the Loki-class and the first
**Partial Manifestation**; first Gate; first calculated kill (the Tallyman); register
as a "weak E-rank"; end on the first taste of full transformation and its backlash.

- **Ch.1 — The Hollows.** Xu Qing wakes in Seo Cheong's beaten body in the slum rain;
  the merge of two hard lives; the dead mother's debt; the System's first window and
  the brand **[Loki — Giant Prince of Elbaf]**; the **Voice of Elbaf** stirs. He does
  the one thing his soul always does first: *takes inventory and finds the exits.*
  *Turn:* he understands what he is and refuses to be glad of it. *Hook:* the loan-gang
  comes for "Seo Cheong's" debt at dawn.
- **Ch.2 — Arithmetic.** The world's rules absorbed cold: Gates, ranks, the
  Association, mana. He hides everything. First **`[Partial Manifestation]`** — a single
  giant hand in a dark alley — tested in secret, with its price (mana-burn, the bone-
  deep wrongness of a body that wants to be larger). *Hook:* to eat, to arm himself, he
  needs to enter an unregistered low Gate the gang controls.
- **Ch.3 — The Tallyman of the Hollows.** Bae Doek-su's loan-gang and the cruelty of
  the awakened underclass; Xu Qing reads the **hierarchy** with the **Eyes of the
  Crossed Pupil**; he does not strike yet — he *measures*. *Hook:* the gang drives
  scavengers into the low Gate as bait; Xu Qing goes in among them.
- **Ch.4 — First Gate.** A low E-rank dungeon; first monster kills with cold knife-work
  and one decisive giant fist; the System's levels and the seductive math of the Loki-
  class; the first **Giant Feat** offered — *survive what was meant to kill you.*
- **Ch.5 — The Feat.** A dungeon hazard / a mid-boss far above an E-rank; Xu Qing
  attempts his first true **Giant Feat** — partial growth held past safe limits — and
  pays for it; **permanent** first reward (duration/strength). The mythic weight of the
  thing. *Hook:* he comes out alive and the gang, who sent him to die, see he didn't.
- **Ch.6 — The Quiet Kill.** Xu Qing dismantles the Tallyman's operation the way he
  does everything — patiently, exactly, from the inside; first calculated human kill of
  this life, no theatrics, no mercy wasted, escape route first. The world's predators
  learn what he is the moment before they stop mattering.
- **Ch.7 — Registration.** He turns himself into the Association as an unremarkable
  awakened; **Han Ji-won** appraises him as a "weak E-rank" and is faintly, correctly
  unsettled. Xu Qing chooses the misclassification on purpose — *invisibility is the
  only armor worth wearing.*
- **Ch.8 — The Voice in the Hall of Kings.** First deep trance into the **Elbaf-in-
  dream**: the hall of dead giant kings, the legacy's grief and pride, the Voice naming
  him *little prince* and pushing him toward a throne. Xu Qing refuses it — and learns
  he can *use* it without *obeying* it.
- **Ch.9 — Small.** A low-rank raid party he attaches to (cover, intel, income);
  ordinary hunters; he hides his power and lets others lead; a lesson in the world's
  scale and the Association's politics; the temptation to grow and the discipline not to.
- **Ch.10 — Pressure.** First real use of **`[Sovereign's Presence]`** as a scalpel —
  a flicker that freezes a horde for the half-second he needs; the party survives a
  near-wipe and credits luck. The legacy *loved* the broadcast; he hated being felt.
- **Ch.11 — Backlash.** Forced into his first near-full **`[Colossus Ascension]`** to
  save the only people whose survival serves him — and the brutal cost: the duration
  cliff, the shrinkage-pain, the **backlash window** in which a giant is, for ninety
  seconds, the weakest man alive. He survives by having planned for exactly this.
- **Ch.12 — Frontier.** Aftermath; what one witness saw and what Xu Qing does about a
  witness; the dead mother's photograph; he sets his rule: *grow only for a Feat, only
  when the arithmetic is in my favor, only where no one survives to point.* *Arc hook:*
  the Association flags an E-rank who keeps walking out of A-rank graves.

## ARC 2 — CONTROLLING THE GIANT (Seoul / provinces; mid-rank Gates) — ch. 13–24
Goal: master partial vs. full growth and the presence; build a tiny, deniable power
base; clear low-to-mid Gates by *calculated* growth; the price (the giant becoming the
default) shows its first teeth; mentor-figure **Old Beom**; rings of reputation begin
despite him. Target ~39,600.
- **13 — The Scavenger Crew.** Falls in with **Beom Tae-gyu**'s low-Gate scrap crew;
  the craft of fighting *smart and small*; cover that pays. *Hook:* a "dead" Gate isn't.
- **14 — Graded Growth.** Training the dial: 12m vs. 28m at will; partial-limb economy;
  the **Eyes** mapping monster cores. *Turn:* control as power. *Hook:* a Feat condition
  he didn't ask for appears mid-fight.
- **15 — The Feat of the Broken Gate.** Holds a collapsing Gate mouth alone to let the
  crew out — a **Giant Feat**; permanent duration gain; first whisper of him in hunter
  forums as "the big guy nobody can ID."
- **16–17 — Reputation as a Wound.** A guild scout sniffs around; Xu Qing's allergy to
  fame vs. the legacy's hunger for it; he sabotages his own legend, badly, twice.
- **18 — Old Beom's War.** The mentor's broken past (a B-rank who grew careless);
  mirror/warning; the conscience Xu Qing keeps at arm's length speaks plainly.
- **19–20 — The Default Creeps.** The price bites: a night he wakes *already partly
  giant*, the body choosing scale on its own; sleep, warmth, human reflex eroding. He
  builds a discipline regimen against his own class. Cold horror, quietly handled.
- **21–22 — The Mid-Rank Trial.** A C/B-rank Gate run as a deliberate **Giant Feat**;
  the crew nearly dies; Xu Qing must choose between cover and saving them — chooses
  *usefully*, and the choice costs his anonymity a notch.
- **23 — Han Ji-won Closes In.** The appraiser confronts the "E-rank" with data that
  doesn't add up; Xu Qing turns interrogation into alliance-of-convenience.
- **24 — The Name He Didn't Pick.** The forums name him — *"the Giant," "Elbaf's
  Shadow," "the Growing Hunter."* He hates it. *Arc hook:* the Association wants a
  reassessment, and a real guild wants him recruited or removed.

## ARC 3 — THE GROWING HUNTER (Korean hunter society; rising) — ch. 25–38
Goal: forced up from the underclass into the real hunter ecosystem; guilds, the
reassessment, the politics of being *unclassifiable*; bigger Feats and clearer giant
power; he learns to move among S-rank scale without being owned; the legacy gains
ground; ends poised to intersect canon. Target ~47,600.
- **25 — Reassessment.** The Association re-tests him; he gives them exactly enough and
  no more; "low D-rank, anomalous." *Hook:* a guild war wants a deniable monster.
- **26–27 — The Recruiters.** White Tiger / Hunters Guild-adjacent overtures; Choi
  Jong-In-style and Baek Yoon-Ho-style approaches; Xu Qing plays them against each
  other to stay independent.
- **28–29 — The Feat of the Drowned Gate.** A flooded high-rank Gate; full **Colossus
  Ascension** used surgically; a **Giant Feat** that unlocks **`[King's Coating]`**.
- **30 — `[Sovereign's Presence]` Matures.** Area morale-break vs. a horde; the
  difference between a king who roars and a survivor who whispers.
- **31–32 — Old Beom's Last Run.** The mentor's exit (death or crippling-for-good);
  Xu Qing's grief expressed the only way he allows — as a *debt to collect*; the
  legacy offers comfort-by-throne and he refuses it again, harder.
- **33–34 — Dungeon Break.** A break in a Seoul district; Xu Qing ends it alone as a
  public, undeniable giant for ninety terrible seconds; the **backlash window** nearly
  kills him; the city has footage now. Fame as a wound, fully inflicted.
- **35–36 — Aftermath & the Eyes Deepen.** Association/Go Gun-Hee take direct interest;
  the **Eyes of the Crossed Pupil** show him something *wrong* in the world's mana — a
  first thread to the **timescape**.
- **37 — Han Ji-won's Choice.** She covers for him at real cost; the alliance becomes
  something like trust; Xu Qing notes, coldly, that he now has something to lose.
- **38 — The Other Anomaly.** First distant brush with **Sung Jin-Woo**'s rising legend
  — the man who walks out of double dungeons; Xu Qing's **Eyes** catch *something
  enormous carried in something quiet.* *Arc hook:* their paths must cross.

## ARC 4 — TWO ANOMALIES (canon integration; Sung Jin-Woo) — ch. 39–50
Goal: the midpoint. Xu Qing and Sung Jin-Woo meet; two growing legends, opposite
methods (shadow army vs. literal giant); recognition, wariness, an uneasy parallel;
a shared threat forces a temporary alliance; the world stops being a frontier Xu Qing
can hide on — *scale has found him.* Target ~42,000.
- **39 — Crossed Paths.** A joint/overlapping raid throws them together; Jin-Woo's
  quiet lethality vs. Xu Qing's quiet caution; each reads the other as *not what the
  System says.*
- **40–41 — Measure for Measure.** They fight *beside* each other against a high-rank
  threat; the giant and the monarch's shadows; mutual, unspoken assessment. Neither
  shows his ceiling.
- **42 — The Shape of Power.** Conversation, spare and loaded; two men who paid prices
  the System hides; Xu Qing recognizes a *kindred discipline* — and a different answer
  to the same question of what to become.
- **43–44 — Go Gun-Hee's Table.** The Association tries to bind both anomalies; Jin-Woo
  and Xu Qing each refuse ownership in their own idiom; a careful, deadly courtesy.
- **45–46 — The Feat of the Demon Castle (analogue).** A brutal instance dungeon run as
  a **Giant Feat**; Xu Qing earns **`[Aura-Limb Projection]`** — the cunning man's
  giant; he and Jin-Woo see each other's *true* scale for the first time. Awe, both ways.
- **47 — Respect Without Trust.** They part as two powers who have decided not to be
  enemies *yet*; Xu Qing files Jin-Woo under *the one variable I cannot model.*
- **48–49 — The Legacy Surges.** Proximity to a being of Jin-Woo's scale wakes the
  **Voice of Elbaf** to its loudest yet; Xu Qing's worst near-loss of self; he holds —
  barely — by the cold rule he forged in Arc 1.
- **50 — Scale Has Found You.** Cosmic notice: a Ruler/Monarch-tier intelligence marks
  the giant; the **timescape** flickers again, *recognizing* him. *Arc hook:* he can no
  longer stay small — only choose *how* to be large.

## ARC 5 — GIANT FEATS & GREAT GATES (large-scale; Jeju & international) — ch. 51–64
Goal: the war of feats. Jeju Island, international Gates, high-rank raids treated as
personal **Giant Feats**; his size and aura change the *scale* of battles; he becomes
a National-tier reality the world must reckon with; the price compounds; the legacy
and the timescape converge. Target ~49,000.
- **51–53 — Jeju (the Ant arc, butterflied).** The S-rank raid on the ant-Gate; Xu
  Qing as a literal counter-titan to the Ant King; full **Colossus Ascension** at
  30m+; the largest **Giant Feat** yet; alongside/against canon S-ranks and Jin-Woo.
- **54 — The King of the Hill.** Survives the Feat's true cost — a backlash so deep he
  is human-fragile for days, hunted by what he humbled; saved by calculation and the
  few allies he permitted himself.
- **55–56 — National Reality.** The world reclassifies him; global S-ranks (Thomas
  Andre-style) test him; Xu Qing refuses both worship and war; *the loudest power
  wielded by the quietest will.*
- **57–58 — The Feat of the Red Gate (analogue).** A timed death-instance; he and a
  small chosen team; he unlocks **`[Elbaf Field]`** — the giant-land domain; the price:
  the field *feels like home*, and home is the throne he keeps refusing.
- **59–60 — International Gates.** Cross-border crises; the politics of a man who can
  level a city to save one; Han Ji-won as his only tether to human scale.
- **61–62 — The Timescape Bleeds.** The **Eyes** and the legacy together reveal it:
  whatever lay *beyond time* in his old world is *leaking* into the world of Gates,
  and the System's makers fear it. The two cosmologies touch. Dread, mythic.
- **63 — The Old Giant's Truth.** The Voice of Elbaf finally tells its whole grief —
  what Elbaf was, what the giant prince Loki lost, why the legacy hungers for a throne;
  Xu Qing understands the *price* fully for the first time.
- **64 — The Bill.** The displaced cost lands where it always meant to: on the warmth
  he guarded. *Arc hook (crisis):* to win what's coming he must become the giant prince
  completely — and that is exactly the thing that would unmake the survivor he is.

## ARC 6 — THE TIMESCAPE & TRANSCENDENCE (cosmic; identity) — ch. 65–74
Goal: the low point and the turn. The threat from *beyond time* fuses with the System's
endgame scale; Xu Qing's worst loss; the **Voice of Elbaf** nearly wins; he descends
into the legacy and the timescape both, and finds the *limit he must choose* to come
back as himself. Greater Feats; the road to **`[True Giant Prince]`**. Target ~36,000.
- **65–66 — The Worst Day.** The crisis breaks: a power he cannot out-calculate; the
  thing he had to lose, lost; Xu Qing's coldest hour, and the temptation to simply
  *become the giant forever* and stop feeling the bill.
- **67–68 — Into Elbaf.** A near-death trance / instance deep into the **Hall of Kings**
  and the legacy's heart; he must out-argue a thousand dead giant princes for the right
  to wield their power *without* their throne.
- **69–70 — The Timescape Within.** The crossed pupils open all the way; he glimpses
  the *outside of time* and his own old death; the two worlds' logics collide; the
  realization that **caution and scale are not opposites** — that the largest move can
  be the most exact.
- **71–72 — Allies, Counted.** Jin-Woo and the few he permitted himself; a reckoning of
  what Xu Qing is *for*, now that he can no longer be only *for himself*; he chooses,
  coldly, to protect — and names it a calculation so he can bear it.
- **73 — The Feat That Cannot Be Survived.** He sets himself an impossible **Giant
  Feat** as the only path to the power the endgame needs; the price is everything.
- **74 — The Limit.** He grasps the shape of **`[True Giant Prince]`**: not bigger —
  *truer*; the fusion of the cold survivor and the mythic giant on *his* terms. *Arc
  hook:* the final threat arrives, at the scale of worlds.

## ARC 7 — THE GIANT PRINCE (climax & resolution) — ch. 75–84
Goal: the final confrontation that *requires* full giant-prince power; won by the limit
he chose to grow, not by raw size; the reconciliation of survivor and legend; the first
true sight of the timescape and the new normal he forges. Target ~37,000.
- **75–76 — The Gathering Scale.** The endgame antagonist (a Monarch/Ruler-tier power
  entangled with the timescape) moves; the world's S-ranks, Jin-Woo, and Xu Qing align
  as the only counter-scale; the calm before a mythic war.
- **77–78 — The War of Giants.** Full-scale battle; Xu Qing at his largest and most
  *exact*; **`[Elbaf Field]`**, **`[King's Coating]`**, aura-limbs, the **Eyes** — every
  tool, every lesson; visceral, terrain-shattering, surgical.
- **79–80 — The Throne Refused.** The antagonist (and the legacy) offer Xu Qing the
  ultimate throne — become the giant prince *absolutely* and win. He refuses absolutism
  and chooses the **limit**; **`[True Giant Prince]`** activates as a *choice*, not a
  surrender. The thematic climax.
- **81 — The Exact Mountain.** He wins the way he always wins — by being, even at the
  scale of a god, the most *precise* thing on the field; the **final Giant Feat**.
- **82 — The Timescape Answered.** The truth of the *light past time* that took him; why
  a giant; what the outside of time wanted; a door closed, or left ajar.
- **83 — The New Normal.** Fallout; what the world makes of the giant who refused the
  throne; Han Ji-won, Jin-Woo, the few who remain; the dead mother's photograph kept.
- **84 — Beyond the Timescape.** Resolution: who Xu Qing chose to be — neither the rat
  of the Hollows nor the throned prince, but a third thing he forged; and the last
  image — the crossed pupils turned, quietly, toward the *outside of time*, which is
  not done with him. *End (with room for a sequel beat).*
