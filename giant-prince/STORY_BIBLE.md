# STORY BIBLE — *Beyond the Timescape: The Giant Prince*

A crossover web novel fusing **Er Gen's *Beyond the Timescape*** (cold, atmospheric,
ruthless-logical cultivation), **Chugong's *Solo Leveling*** (System-powered hunter
action, modern Korea, Gates), and the **mythic scale and warrior-legend tone of *One
Piece*'s Elbaf arc** (Prince Loki, giants, kingly Haki-like presence).

Living reference document. Update the instant a new fact is established in prose.

**Working title:** *Beyond the Timescape: The Giant Prince*
**Alternates:** *Xu Qing: Loki of the Gates* · *Beyond the Timescape: Elbaf's Shadow*
**Target length:** 250,000–300,000 words. ~84 chapters across 7 arcs, ~3,300–3,800
words/chapter, plus a Prologue.
**Rating:** Mature — graphic violence, dark survival themes, a ruthless protagonist.
**Tone keywords:** mythic, fable-like, cold, atmospheric, larger-than-life, calculating.

---

## 0. The Three Bloodlines of the Story (what each source contributes)

- **From *Beyond the Timescape*:** the protagonist himself — Xu Qing — and his soul:
  quiet, cold, serious, supremely cautious, ruthless and decisive toward threats,
  self-disciplined, a pragmatic survivor who lives by calculated risk. His **crossed
  pupils** (the reincarnation marker). The grim, profound, dark-fable *texture* of
  the prose. The cultivation logic of paying a price for every gain.
- **From *Solo Leveling*:** the **world** — modern Korea, the **Gates**, **dungeons**,
  **mana**, the **Hunters Association**, **guilds**, hunter **ranks (E→S→National)**,
  **the System** (windows, quests, stats, penalty zones), **dungeon breaks**, and
  canon figures (Sung Jin-Woo, Go Gun-Hee, the Jeju Island raid, etc.).
- **From *One Piece* (Elbaf / Prince Loki):** the **class** — a giant-prince warrior
  legacy. Size-manipulation, overwhelming physical power, giant weapons, a kingly
  **Conqueror's-Haki-like presence**, and the mythic *weight* of becoming a legend —
  the burden and glory of being a giant among men.

## 1. Logline

A cold, calculating cultivator named **Xu Qing** — who clawed his way to
transcendence in a world *beyond the timescape* and then died crossing into the
nothing past it — is drawn by a **purple light** into the body of a starving
seventeen-year-old in modern Korea. He wakes inside the world of the **Gates**, fused
to a body whose harsh life mirrors his own ruthless soul, and the **System** brands
him with a class no hunter has ever held: the **Loki-class — the Giant Prince of
Elbaf**. He can *grow* — ten, twenty, thirty meters of mythic warrior flesh — and
crush the monsters of the Gates underfoot. But Xu Qing did not survive two worlds by
becoming a giant. He survived by being *small*: patient, hidden, exact. Now the
quietest man alive carries the loudest power in the world, and he intends to wield it
the way he wields everything — coldly, precisely, and only when the arithmetic is in
his favor.

## 2. The Protagonist — Xu Qing (徐青) / "Seo Cheong" (서청)

### 2.1 The soul (kept with perfect fidelity to *Beyond the Timescape*)
- **Personality:** quiet, serious, cold. **Extremely cautious and logical** — he
  plans escape routes before he plans attacks. **Ruthless and decisive toward
  threats:** efficient kills, no theatrical mercy, no gloating, no unnecessary risk.
  Self-disciplined to the bone. A pragmatic survivor who prizes **strength, control,
  and calculated risk** above pride, glory, or applause.
- **What he is NOT:** loud, boastful, rage-driven, prince-like in manner, or hungry
  for an audience. The power he now carries is theatrical; *he is not*. That tension
  is the spine of his characterization. He never performs being a giant. He *uses* it.
- **The crossed pupils:** his eyes carry a faint **cross-shaped split in each pupil**
  — the mark that followed his soul across death. In this world it is both a
  reincarnation marker and the seed of a perception power (see **Eyes of the Crossed
  Pupil**).
- **Interior voice:** spare, exact, grimly dry. He measures everything. He distrusts
  gifts. *A power with no price has simply hidden the bill* is the kind of thing he
  thinks, and he is right.

### 2.2 The vessel (Solo Leveling Korea)
- **Korean name:** **Seo Cheong** (서청) — the body's legal name; 徐(Seo)·青(Cheong)
  deliberately mirrors Xu Qing. The world calls him Seo Cheong; in his own skull he
  remains **Xu Qing**, and the narration favors *Xu Qing*.
- **Age:** 17 at rebirth (the story spans roughly 17 → early 20s).
- **Background (harsh, merges with his soul):** the body's previous owner was a
  destitute, unawakened orphan in a decayed industrial district of Seoul (the
  **Guryong-adjacent slums / the "Hollows"**), buried in his dead mother's hospital
  debt, beaten and used by a low-rank hunter loan-gang. The body died of a beating in
  a back alley; Xu Qing's soul arrived in the same instant the purple light fell.
  The despair and hardness already in that body **merge seamlessly** with Xu Qing's
  cold soul — granting, the System says, the *latent potential of a giant prince*.
- **Appearance:** lean, underfed, then rapidly hardening into something broad and
  dense as the giant legacy roots; black hair, plain dark eyes whose **crossed
  pupils** surface when his power stirs. Unremarkable until he chooses not to be — and
  then he is the largest thing on the battlefield.

### 2.3 Wants & needs
- **Wants:** never again to be at anyone's mercy; to understand the new world's rules
  before it understands him; to find whether the *timescape* — the thing beyond time
  he died chasing — reaches into this world too.
- **Needs (the arc):** to decide how much of the **Loki / giant legacy** he lets
  *become him*. The class wants a king, a legend, a giant who stands in the open. His
  soul wants a survivor in the shadows. The climax forces the reconciliation.

## 3. The Power System — the **Loki-class (Giant Prince of Elbaf)**

The System of Solo Leveling exists exactly as in canon (windows, stats, levels,
quests, penalty zones, the **Reawakening**, the Architect, the Rulers/Monarchs). Xu
Qing is registered by the Association as a hunter; *privately*, the System has bonded
him to a **hidden, custom class** it names:

> **[CLASS: Loki — Giant Prince of Elbaf]** *(Unique / Legacy class)*

He treats the class, in his own mind, the way a *Beyond the Timescape* cultivator
treats a **dao**: a thing to be *digested* a tier at a time, each tier paid for.

### 3.1 Core faculties (unlock progressively with level + Giant Feats)

1. **Giant Form / Size Manipulation** — *the signature.*
   - **`[Partial Manifestation]`**: grow a single limb or region to giant scale (a
     giant arm, a giant fist, a colossal stride). Cheap, fast, controllable. His
     bread-and-butter early on — and forever his most *tactical* tool, because it
     fits his cautious style: localized power, minimal exposure.
   - **`[Colossus Ascension]`**: full transformation, **10–30+ meters** by mastery.
     Immense strength, reach, durability, terrain-altering force. **Early limits:**
     short duration (seconds → minutes), heavy mana cost, and **backlash** (muscle
     tearing, mana-burn, a cooldown of crushing fatigue and shrinkage-pain). Mastery
     buys duration, control, and partial/graded growth (12m vs. 28m at will).
   - **Hard rule:** size is **not free**. Mass must be paid for in mana and in bodily
     toll. Over-growing past his current mastery threshold risks **rupture** (the
     giant equivalent of over-ringing) — exactly the kind of cliff Xu Qing refuses to
     walk off without arithmetic.

2. **Giant's Might & Weapon Proficiency** — **`[Giant's Might]`**
   - Superhuman base stats that **scale dramatically** with size. Strikes carry
     **"giant force"** that partially ignores armor, barriers, and defensive skills
     (it doesn't bypass them — it *out-masses* them).
   - **Giant-weapon proficiency:** clubs, axes, mauls, and especially **improvised
     gate-debris** (a snapped pillar, a torn dungeon gate, a boulder, a monster's own
     severed limb). He can also fight with **refined technique at normal size** — the
     cold knife-work of his old life — which keeps him lethal when growing is unwise.

3. **Royal / Conqueror's Presence** — **`[Sovereign's Presence]`** (Haki-analogue)
   - A kingly, domineering aura that **intimidates, paralyzes, or crushes the will**
     of weaker monsters and lower-rank hunters. Scales from a pressure that makes
     beasts cringe → **area control / morale-break** → a **`[King's Coating]`** that
     sheathes his strikes and skin in invisible force (armament/conqueror analogue),
     letting him strike "the thing inside the thing."
   - **Characterization note:** Loki's class *wants* him to broadcast this like a
     prince on a throne. Xu Qing uses it like a scalpel — a flicker to make a horde
     hesitate for the half-second he needs, rarely a roar.

4. **Giant Feats / Prince's Trials** — the growth engine (**bold** in prose).
   - The System issues, or Xu Qing *recognizes*, **monumental conditions**: survive
     impossible odds, fell a foe far above his rank, hold a collapsing Gate, end a
     dungeon break alone. Completing one is a **Giant Feat** and grants **permanent**
     rewards: new giant techniques, longer/larger transformation, stronger aura,
     stat leaps, or evolutions of the class.
   - This mechanic is *tailor-made* for Xu Qing's goal-oriented, cautious mind: he
     treats major Gates not as glory but as **calculated feats** — high cost, high,
     *permanent* yield — to be attempted only when prepared. The narration treats
     each Feat with mythic weight, like a hero-trial in a saga.

5. **Titan's Endurance** — **`[Titan's Endurance]`**
   - High base toughness and **regeneration** that improves enormously in giant form;
     he can tank blows that would kill an ordinary A-rank. Recovery is fast but **not
     infinite** — and the *backlash* of dropping out of full giant form is a real
     window of weakness he must always cover.

6. **Eyes of the Crossed Pupil** — **`[Eyes of the Crossed Pupil]`**
   - His *Beyond the Timescape* crossed pupils, reborn as perception: he reads
     **structural weak points** (of monsters, barriers, terrain, and even the giant
     architecture of a Gate), **mana flows**, and a **"royal insight"** into enemy
     **hierarchies** — which monster is the true core, which hunter truly commands,
     where a horde's will actually hangs. Lets a giant fight like a **surgeon**.

### 3.2 Later evolutions (named/teased; earned in Arcs 5–7)
- **`[Aura-Limb Projection]`** — conjuring translucent giant limbs (a fist, a stride)
  without full transformation: the cunning man's giant.
- **`[Elbaf Field]`** — a localized "giant-land" domain that warps scale, weight, and
  presence in an area; allies feel larger and braver, enemies smaller and slower.
- **`[True Giant Prince]`** — the perfected state that fuses his **calculating soul**
  with **mythic giant power**: full control, no backlash, conceptual size — the
  climax-tier form, and a *choice* as much as a power.

### 3.3 The price (the story's spine — *Beyond the Timescape* logic)
The class brands itself "no drawback" the way Solo Leveling gifts often seem to be.
Xu Qing does not believe it for a second, and he is right. The bills, seeded early
and revealed across the arcs:
- **The body remembers being small.** Every meter of growth he has not *earned*
  through a Feat erodes something — sleep, warmth, the human scale of his own
  reflexes. Ungoverned growth threatens to make the **giant the default and the man
  the costume.**
- **Legacy hungers for a throne.** The Loki legacy carries a *will* — a giant
  prince's pride and grief, an echo of Elbaf — that pushes him to stand tall, be
  seen, be a king. To Xu Qing, who survives by being unseen, **fame is a wound**, and
  the class keeps trying to inflict it.
- **Scale draws scale.** A man who can become a mountain is *noticed* by mountains —
  the Rulers, the Monarchs, the powers above the System, and whatever the **timescape**
  is, all turn toward a thing this large. Growing solves problems by creating larger
  ones. Calculated risk is the only way he survives the attention his own power buys.

## 4. Cast

### 4.1 Original
- **Xu Qing / Seo Cheong** — protagonist (see §2).
- **Mother (deceased), "Ma Seon-i" / the body's mother** — her hospital debt is the
  body's origin wound; her memory is the one warm thing Xu Qing inherits and guards.
  Used sparingly; she is the human anchor the giant legacy keeps trying to burn off.
- **Bae Doek-su / "the Tallyman of the Hollows"** — low-rank hunter loan-shark who
  ran the gang that beat the body to death; Xu Qing's **first calculated kill** in
  this world and first taste of how this world's predators work. *(Arc 1.)*
- **Han Ji-won** — a pragmatic, mid-rank Association appraiser/handler who first
  registers the strange E-rank that keeps surviving the unsurvivable; becomes Xu
  Qing's wary, useful conduit to the Association and a mirror of normal human caution
  beside his inhuman kind. *(Arc 1–3.)*
- **"Old Beom" / Beom Tae-gyu** — a washed-out, crippled former B-rank who runs a
  scavenger crew in low-rank Gates; the closest thing Xu Qing has to a craft-mentor
  for *fighting smart and small*; a quiet conscience he keeps at arm's length.
  *(Arc 2.)*
- **The Voice of Elbaf / "the Old Giant"** — the dormant *will* inside the Loki-class,
  surfacing in trance, dream, and Feat: a giant prince's pride, grief, and counsel.
  Not a separate person so much as the legacy's *pressure*. The story's antagonist of
  identity — what Xu Qing must master without becoming. *(Throughout.)*

### 4.2 Canon (Solo Leveling)
- **Sung Jin-Woo** — the Shadow Monarch's vessel. Introduced Arc 4. **Two growing
  legends, opposite in method:** Jin-Woo commands an *army of the dead* and grows by
  hunting *down* (becoming death); Xu Qing grows by hunting *up* and becoming *large*.
  Mutual recognition between two anomalies the System should not contain — wary
  respect, an uneasy parallel, a possible alliance against a shared scale of threat.
  Xu Qing reads, with his crossed pupils, that Jin-Woo is *carrying something
  enormous in something quiet* — and recognizes a kindred discipline.
- **Go Gun-Hee** — chairman of the Korean Hunters Association; the watchful state
  power that *must* classify the man who can become a giant. Sharp, kindly, deadly.
- **Cha Hae-In, Baek Yoon-Ho (Iron/White Tiger Guild), Choi Jong-In (Hunters
  Guild), Thomas Andre & global S-ranks** — the hunter ecosystem Xu Qing must move
  through without being *owned*. Jeju Island, the Ant King/Beru event, international
  Gates as **Giant Feats**. *(Arcs 3–6; timeline butterflies acknowledged.)*
- **The Rulers & Monarchs / the Architect / the System's makers** — the cosmic scale
  Solo Leveling's endgame runs on; Xu Qing's giant legacy and the **timescape** are
  woven into this in Arcs 6–7.

## 5. World Rules (hard limits — do not bend for plot convenience)

1. **Solo Leveling canon physics hold:** Gates, mana, ranks E–S (+ National-level),
   dungeon breaks, magic beasts, the System's quests/penalties, the Reawakening test.
2. **Size is paid for.** Growth costs mana + bodily toll; over-growth past mastery
   risks rupture; full **Colossus Ascension** has a hard duration cap that only Feats
   extend; dropping out of it leaves a real **backlash window**.
3. **Giant Feats are earned, not farmed.** Each is a genuine, dangerous, *singular*
   accomplishment. They cannot be repeated for the same reward; they are the only
   source of *permanent* class evolution. (Keeps stakes real and progress mythic.)
4. **Presence affects will, not facts.** `[Sovereign's Presence]` bends courage,
   coordination, and morale — it does not delete an enemy's power or rewrite physics.
5. **The price is real and accumulating** (see §3.3). "No drawback" is a lie the
   story slowly exposes; the displaced cost lands on Xu Qing's *human scale* and on
   the *attention* his power draws. Fixed law, not vibe.
6. **The System is not omniscient.** It misclassifies Xu Qing as an unremarkable
   E-rank far longer than it should, because nothing in its tables has a row for a
   man who is two souls and a giant. The frontier of its ignorance is his early edge.
7. **The timescape is *outside*.** Whatever lay beyond time in his old world is not
   bound by this world's System; its touch here is rare, wrong-feeling, and ominous.

## 8. Style & Craft Rules
- **Prose:** epic, fable-like, mythic — grand, legendary atmosphere with One Piece
  scale, *Beyond the Timescape*'s dark survival cold, and Solo Leveling's clean action.
  Short, weighted sentences for menace; long rolling ones for giant-scale spectacle.
- **POV:** tight third on Xu Qing, with **heavy cold interior monologue** (*italic*
  for direct thought). Occasional brief outside-POV interludes (an Association
  appraiser, a terrified hunter, Jin-Woo) to show how the world *reads* the giant.
- **Fights:** visceral, tactical, two-layered — clever small-scale knife-logic AND
  overwhelming giant-scale destruction in the same scene. He *thinks* even at 25m.
- **Formatting:** `# Chapter X: Title`; *italics* for thoughts; **bold** for skill
  names and **Giant Feats**; System windows in fenced blocks or bracketed lines.
- **Keep Xu Qing in character always:** he never becomes loud or princely in
  personality. Power is a tool. Mercy, when it appears, is a *calculation* or a
  hard-won *choice* — never a reflex.
